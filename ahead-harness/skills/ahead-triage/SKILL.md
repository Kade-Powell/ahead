# AHEAD triage

Read [../references/human-led-policy.md](../references/human-led-policy.md)
before proceeding.

Ask only the minimum questions needed to distinguish a product change,
investigation, corrective debugging, operational stabilization or missing
information. Preserve the reporter's wording and separate facts from guesses.

Return a bounded triage note with:

- expected versus observed behavior;
- affected scope, user impact and urgency;
- reproduction status and attached evidence;
- likely work kind, without claiming root cause;
- missing information and the recommended next task.

Do not edit code, label a cause as confirmed, or launch the heavy diagnosis
workflow until the human accepts the next step.
